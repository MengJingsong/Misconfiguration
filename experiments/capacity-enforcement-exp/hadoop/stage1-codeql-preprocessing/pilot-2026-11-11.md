# Stage-1 extension — Hadoop 3.4.2 (2026-11-11)

The stage-1 queries beyond `if`, run on Hadoop after the Cassandra pilot
([`../../cassandra/stage1-codeql-preprocessing/pilot-2026-11-11.md`](../../cassandra/stage1-codeql-preprocessing/pilot-2026-11-11.md)),
with counted `for` loops already dropped. These are stage-1 rows only; nothing is banded yet.

- **Queries:** `codeql-queries/hadoop/queries/capacity-enforcement-exp/EnforcementPlaces.ql` and
  `CapacitySetPlaces.ql`. They are copies of the Cassandra queries plus the `moduleDir` column, with the
  same predicates (`common/lib/CapacityEnforcementCore.qll`) and Hadoop's scope (`hadoop/ifcheck/IfCheck.qll`).
- **Run:** CodeQL 2.27.0, `codeql-dbs/hadoop-db`, 32 cores. The enforcement query took about 10 min
  (Cassandra: 2), the capacity-set query 1 min. The four `if` queries were re-run and still give
  9,269 + 1,573 rows. Results are gitignored (`codeql-queries/results/hadoop/`).
- **Numbers:** `../../cassandra/stage1-codeql-preprocessing/pilot_report.py --system hadoop --list`.

## 4. Stage-2 volume

`make-stage2-batches.py results/hadoop <out> 200 --extension` builds **10,008 units** (3,677
enforcement, 1,194 verdict, 5,119 capacity-set), or **50 batches** of 200. Cassandra had 6,439 units

## 5. Decisions before stage 2

1. **Anchors.** The batches would carry Hadoop's 18 `if` anchors, plus the two Hadoop true positives
   in §3 as extension anchors (both capacity-set rows). Hadoop has no verified enforcement-form
   anchor. Proposal: also carry Cassandra's 6 extension anchors as cross-system calibration rows.
2. **Asserts:** keep the rows for stage 2 to band D (proposed), or drop them for Hadoop in stage 1.
3. **Where the rows go:** new A–C units at the end of Jingsong's `queue.csv`, or a separate
   `writeup-set-ext.csv` as for Cassandra. New D units: left in `bands.csv` unread, as for Cassandra,
   unless Yang's re-check of band D is to take them.
