# OOM lead list — leads from phase 1 (Hadoop)

Started 2026-11-17. One row per **OOM lead** from a Hadoop stage-3 case: section E of an autonomous
case ([`playbook-autonomous.md`](playbook-autonomous.md), "OOM leads"), or a recheck of a filed case
("Recheck mode"). Shared by both people: append rows only, never edit another person's row. The
session that files a case copies its section E rows here. A lead says real memory can exceed what a
constraint governs; it is not a result. Hadoop has no OOM-exp phase yet; when it starts, a queuing
agent takes candidates from here under the [queuing playbook](../../../OOM-exp/queuing-playbook.md).
Format and statuses as in the Cassandra list,
[`../../cassandra/stage3-ai-deep-read/oom-lead-list.md`](../../cassandra/stage3-ai-deep-read/oom-lead-list.md).
