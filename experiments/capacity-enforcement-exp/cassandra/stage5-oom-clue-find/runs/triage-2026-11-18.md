6. Sizing assumptions. I used a 2 GiB heap: memtable pool 512 MiB with flush at about 170 MiB, heap/10 is about 204 MiB and heap/40 about 51 MiB, and native_transport_max_message_size is 16 MiB. For the direct budget I used about heap/2 per jvm-server.options; cassandra-env.sh gives RAM/4 instead. Most magnitudes are the agents' estimates. Only OC-282 (145 MiB at 1 KB keys), OC-322 (a 512 MB node killed), OC-323 (2.6x the pool), OC-404 (about 255-317 MB for 500 tables), OC-269 (exactly C x 1 MiB) and OC-147 (0 refusals) have stage-4 measurements.
