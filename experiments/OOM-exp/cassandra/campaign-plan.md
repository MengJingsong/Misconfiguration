## Prioritized queue

**How a lead becomes a row** (screening, the JIRA check, IDs, priority, row format, the gate before a run, row
statuses): `../queuing-playbook.md` (retired 2026-11-17) (2026-11-17), which merges the rules below and elsewhere.

**JIRA check before a candidate is queued (rule, 2026-11-16).** Before a new row is added to the queue (or a
lead is picked for a batch), search CASSANDRA JIRA for its mechanism, starting from
[`leads/jira-oom-survey.csv`](../../capacity-enforcement-exp/cassandra/stage5-oom-clue-find/jira-oom-survey.csv) once it exists, and record the matching issues (or "none
found", with the query) in the row's `source:` / hypothesis text. A mechanism already reported and fixed is queued as a
**regression check** or a **positive control**, not as a new lead; one reported and closed as by design is queued
only if the case can show a broken or misleading documented contract. A verified case gets the same check to
record its prior art. **(Researcher, 2026-11-17:) the JIRA check serves to find OOM-exp candidates; no comments or
reports are posted to JIRA.** From 2026-11-17 rows are queued by the queuing agent under
`../queuing-playbook.md` (retired 2026-11-17) (owner's ruling).

Direction/sub-direction names are from [`../README.md`](../README.md#two-directions):
convergence sub-directions are *multiplicity*, *concurrency/surge*,
*replacement-overlap* (and the done *standing-pool* shape); bypass sub-directions
are the mechanism rows of the bypass playbook (here *release-defect*, *accounting
mismatch*, *failed-reservation*, *pool-fallback*). Rows are ordered by priority. The
sub-direction is the hypothesis chosen up front; a case is separately tagged
**mixed** if the evidence crosses directions (see the C4/B5 row).
