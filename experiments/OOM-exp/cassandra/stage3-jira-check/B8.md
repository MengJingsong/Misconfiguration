# B8 — stage 3 JIRA check (2026-11-23)

**Case:** [`commitlog_max_outstanding_replay_bytes--accounting-mismatch`](../cases/bypass/commitlog_max_outstanding_replay_bytes-accounting-mismatch/commitlog_max_outstanding_replay_bytes--accounting-mismatch.md)
(true positive; deciding condition S `verified-OOM · diagnostic`, 1 GiB, restore emulation + injected flush stall).

**Mechanism checked (§4).** `CommitLogReplayer.handleMutation` bounds the replay queue by count (1,024) and by
`pendingMutationBytes` = **serialized** entry size (64 MiB, `cassandra.commitlog_max_outstanding_replay_bytes`), while each
queued deserialized tiny-cell mutation holds ≈ 15× its serialized size on heap (0.95 GiB at the bound). When the replay volume
exceeds the memtable pool and the appliers stall, the queue fills to its bound and a 1 GiB node OOMs during start-up.

**Label: `regression` (exact: CASSANDRA-8639)** — qualified: an **incomplete fix**, not a code regression. 8639 reported this
holder and trigger (CL replay of dense mutations OOMs the node); its committed fix (in 5.0.9) bounds the queue by serialized bytes,
not by the heap size the issue proposed, so the mechanism persists at ≈ 15× the nominal bound. Neither "the mechanism came back"
nor "the case's root cause is wrong" fits exactly; owner to confirm the label.

## Reason

CASSANDRA-8639 is the same holder and trigger: replay of dense mutations, where the bound on in-flight replay mutations does
not reflect their heap size. Its fix is in 5.0.9 (`git merge-base --is-ancestor 1c41a9ac2c cassandra-5.0.9` → true), but it chose
the serialized size as the unit, which is the gap B8 measures (15.0× for 5,900-cell maps, 2.2× for blobs). So the mechanism was
reported and "fixed", and the fix is incomplete for tiny-cell data. This differs from the 2026-11-16 campaign-plan reading ("no open
issue on the unit"): under the stage-3 rules a fixed exact issue with its fix in the pinned version is labelled `regression`, here
in the sense of a residual of an incomplete fix (the case's root cause is not wrong). The re-check found no newer issue (2016–2026)
on replay memory, the replay byte unit, or a replay OOM with this holder; CASSANDRA-16878 touches the same code for ordering only.
