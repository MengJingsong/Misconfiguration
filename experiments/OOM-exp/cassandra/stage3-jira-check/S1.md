(verdict true positive; S3@768 `verified-OOM · deployment`).

**Mechanism checked (case §4).** A joint direct-memory endpoint whose decisive, uncontrolled holder is the in-flight
`DataOutputBuffer` scratch of large mutations: each of the 32 `MutationStage` threads grows its thread-local scratch buffer
(direct by default since CASSANDRA-16471) to 15–30 MiB in `CommitLog.add` → `expandToFit` for 15 MiB mutations
(within the default `max_mutation_size`); `dob_max_recycle_bytes` trims only after use. Stacked on the receive-queue
reserve (C1), hint slabs and the networking pool at their caps, the sum exceeds `MaxDirectMemorySize` (768 MiB).

**Label: `known-open (partial: CASSANDRA-21656)`**
