# B6 — stage 3 JIRA check (2026-11-23)

**Case file:** [`../cases/bypass/internode_application_queue_capacity-accounting-mismatch/internode_application_queue_capacity--accounting-mismatch.md`](../cases/bypass/internode_application_queue_capacity-accounting-mismatch/internode_application_queue_capacity--accounting-mismatch.md)
(verdict true positive; B and D `verified-OOM · diagnostic`, A `verified-gap · service-loss · deployment`).

**Mechanism checked (case §4).** The internode send and receive application queues
(`internode_application_*_queue_*capacity`) charge messages by **on-wire** (`serializedSize`) bytes, but queued writes
live on the heap as deserialized objects: a 1000-tiny-cell map write is 10.8 KiB on the wire and ≈ 213 / 181 KiB on the
coordinator / replica heap (≈ 17–20×), so queues within their limits can pin many GiB of heap. The coordinator also
retains each sent `Mutation` (`hintOnFailure`) after flush, charged to no sender counter. Heap OOM / Full-GC service loss.

**Label: `new`**

No issue reports that a queue within its on-wire limit can hold many times that on the heap, or that the coordinator's
after-flush `hintOnFailure` copy is charged to no counter.

**Documented:** no. `conf/cassandra.yaml:1371-1372` states the unit — "These limits are applied to the on-wire size of
the message being sent or received." — and l. 1375-1377 the per-link multiplicity, but nothing warns that the limits do
not bound heap or that the heap cost can be ≈ 20× the on-wire size; no text in `doc/`, `NEWS.txt`.

**Reason.** The counter behaves as documented (on-wire bytes), so B6 is a documented unit that does not bound heap plus an
uncovered after-flush retention. JIRA has the control's origin (15066), the same ratio fixed on a different path (8639),
and the retention acknowledged in a since-removed design (9318), but no issue with this holder and trigger or the OOM
symptom on the internode queues. All key queries from the 2026-11-16 check were rerun with the same or higher hit counts
and no new relevant hit; the sweep of issues filed since 2026-11-10 found nothing on point.
