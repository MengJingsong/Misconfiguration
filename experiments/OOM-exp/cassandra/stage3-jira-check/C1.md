# C1 — stage 3 JIRA check (2026-11-23)

**Case file:** [`../cases/convergence/internode_application_receive_queue_capacity-multiplicity/internode_application_receive_queue_capacity--multiplicity--run4.md`](../cases/convergence/internode_application_receive_queue_capacity-multiplicity/internode_application_receive_queue_capacity--multiplicity--run4.md)
(verdict true positive; D and H `verified-OOM · diagnostic`).

**Mechanism checked (case §4).** Queued LARGE internode frames wait in **direct** memory (`InboundMessageHandler$LargeMessage.buffers`)
until a `MutationStage` thread deserializes them. With ≥ 4 loaded peers the receive queue admits N·Q + G = 528–544 MiB
(N·E filling the 512 MiB global reserve G), every per-peer and global counter within contract; that total plus ≈ 50 MiB
baseline and the networking pool exceeds a 512–640 MiB `MaxDirectMemorySize` → `OutOfMemoryError: Direct buffer memory`.
Nothing reconciles the receive reserves with the JVM direct limit.

**Label: `new`**

**Documented:** no. `conf/cassandra.yaml:1375-1377` documents the multiplicity in on-wire bytes ("any given node may
have a maximum of N*3*(internode_application_send_queue_capacity+internode_application_receive_queue_capacity) messages
queued without any coordination between them"), but not that queued receive frames are direct memory or that G (+ N·Q)
must fit under `MaxDirectMemorySize`; `cassandra-env.sh` sizes the direct limit (l. 58–66) without reference to it.

**Reason.** No issue reports a direct-memory OOM from queued internode receive frames, nor a check of the receive reserves
against `MaxDirectMemorySize`. The direct-OOM reports in JIRA (21529, 19535, 18762, 17025, 16154) name other holders or
none, and 15358 only ensured the frames are direct. All key 2026-11-16 queries rerun; no new relevant hit, and 21529 is
unchanged since 2026-08-12. Low priority: the failing direct budgets (512–640 MiB) are below what `cassandra-env.sh`
resolves for any node, as the case says.
