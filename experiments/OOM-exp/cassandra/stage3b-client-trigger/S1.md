Analysis only (no node run). Code references are to the pinned 5.0.9 tree `systems/cassandra/source/`, paths below
`src/java/org/apache/cassandra/` unless a file under `conf/` is named.

**Mechanism.** Each of the 32 `MutationStage` threads grows its thread-local direct `DataOutputBuffer` to 15–30 MiB when
`CommitLog.add` serializes a 15 MiB mutation (`db/commitlog/CommitLog.java:306-309`), uncounted; stacked on the C1
receive-queue reserve (8 loaded peers), hint slabs and the networking pool at their caps, the sum exceeds a 768 MiB
`MaxDirectMemorySize` → direct OOM.

**Condition assessed:** **S3@768** (`verified-OOM · deployment`): heap 2 GiB, direct 768 MiB, 8 coordinators sending
15 MiB blobs to the victim replica at CL=ONE, plus a hint/DOB layer (a client writing to a keyspace whose other replica
Dn is down) and `nodetool flush`/`compact` at 20/35 s. Each ingredient removal survives (joint endpoint).

**Label: `client-triggerable given an operator-set MaxDirectMemorySize ≤ 768 MiB and a down replica · data role; a replica steerable by key`**

Reach: given conditions (a direct limit set below the auto value — rare; one node down for the hint layer —
occasional/routine). Lowest privilege: **data role** (MODIFY on blob tables, one of them in a keyspace with a replica on
the down node).

## Condition and how common

- `MaxDirectMemorySize` ≤ 768 MiB set by the operator with a 2 GiB heap: **rare** (below the auto value; hand-sized
  containers).
- One replica of the hint keyspace down during the attack: **occasional to routine** (failures, rolling restarts).
- Maintenance overlap (flush/compaction): routine under load, but the verified arm used admin-issued nodetool commands.

## Blast radius

- **Which node:** the victim replica, chosen by key; reached through 8 other coordinators. One coordinator (C3) also died
  of a heap OOM in a control arm (B6-like retention of 1 MiB writes), so coordinators are not entirely safe.
- **Repeatable:** on every node with such a direct limit.
- **Other tenants:** the victim is killed; one replica lost per affected replica set; with Dn already down, a replica set
  that contains both Dn and the victim loses two members (QUORUM at RF 3 unavailable for those ranges).

## Operator visibility

Large mutations are visible in table metrics and in the commit log; `internode_inbound` near its ceiling; hints piling
up for Dn. No log warning for 15 MiB mutations at defaults. After: `OutOfMemoryError: Direct buffer memory` on
`MutationStage` in `CommitLog.add` → `expandToFit` (3/3). Attribution to a role only via `system_views.clients` or the
audit log (off by default).

## 3-b confirm

Not worthwhile now: two operator-side conditions (a sub-default direct limit, a down replica) and an admin step in the
verified arm. A confirm would first need a redesign without nodetool (natural flush/compaction) to remove the admin step;
the client side (MODIFY, 15 MiB blobs) is not in doubt.

## Reason

The client contributions — 15 MiB blob writes (within `max_mutation_size` and the frame limit), CL=ONE, routing through
8 coordinators to keys the victim owns, a second write stream into a keyspace with a down replica — need only MODIFY
(`ModificationStatement.java:249`). Three steps of the verified arm are outside the client: the 768 MiB direct limit
(operator, below the auto value), the down node Dn that makes the hint layer exist, and the `nodetool flush`/`compact`
commands (admin as run; natural maintenance is a plausible but untested substitute, and the layers-off arm survived). The
label therefore names the two operator-side conditions; at any budget the start-up script resolves the stack did not
fail (0/5 above 768 MiB).
