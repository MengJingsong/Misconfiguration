**Mechanism.** Queued LARGE internode frames wait in direct memory until a `MutationStage` thread deserializes them.
With ≥ 4 loaded coordinators the victim replica's receive queue admits N·Q + G = 528–544 MiB, every counter within
contract; that plus ≈ 50 MiB baseline and pool slack exceeds a 512–640 MiB `MaxDirectMemorySize` → direct OOM.

**Condition assessed:** **D** (`verified-OOM · diagnostic`): shipped yaml, heap 2 GiB, direct 512 MiB (largest failing
640 MiB, 4/5 in H), 8 coordinators writing 1 MiB blobs at CL=ONE, 500/s total, no Byteman, default timeouts. H (hints off)
is the same with a supporting deviation.

**Label: `client-triggerable given an operator-set MaxDirectMemorySize ≤ 640 MiB · data role; a replica steerable by key, repeatable on every node so configured`**

Reach: given a condition (a direct limit set below what `cassandra-env.sh` resolves: rare). Lowest privilege: **data
role** (MODIFY on a table with a blob/text column).

## Condition and how common

An operator who sets `MAX_DIRECT_MEMORY_SIZE` (or `-XX:MaxDirectMemorySize`) to ≤ 640 MiB with a ≈ 2 GiB heap: **rare**.
The default start-up script never produces it, and CASSANDRA-20296 (in 5.0.9) sets heap_limit/2 or ¼ RAM. Container
deployments that hand-size a small direct limit are the plausible case. At any auto-resolved budget the client's load
does not kill the node (largest failing 640 MiB).

## Blast radius

- **Which node:** a replica (not a coordinator) of the keys the attacker writes; the attacker chooses it by key and routes
  the writes through ≥ 4 other coordinators. Senders and the co-replica stayed healthy (in this run, post-endpoint, hints
  for the dead victim filled the harness's tmpfs — an artifact).
- **Repeatable:** on every node with such a direct limit, by moving the key range (≈ 15–32 s per node).
- **Other tenants:** the victim is killed (`kill -9` after the forced heap OOM); its replica sets lose one member (RF 3 /
  QUORUM still served); with hints on, coordinators accumulate hints for it.

## Operator visibility

Before: `system_views.internode_inbound` `using_bytes` and throttled counts on the victim near their ceilings; dropped
mutations; large-mutation traffic visible in table write metrics. No log warning at defaults. After: `OutOfMemoryError:
Direct buffer memory` on whichever thread allocates last (the holder is `LargeMessage.buffers` in the dump). Attribution
to a role needs `system_views.clients` on the coordinators (with authentication) or the audit log (off by default).

## 3-b confirm

Feasible (the stage-2 harness runs on one host) but low value: the decisive condition is the operator's direct limit, and
the client side is plain MODIFY traffic. If run: condition D topology, `PasswordAuthenticator` + `CassandraAuthorizer`,
attacker role with MODIFY on the blob table only, a victim role on another table reading/writing at QUORUM, direct
512 MiB. Lower priority than C2, S2, C9.

## Reason

All client steps — 1 MiB blob inserts at CL=ONE, routed through ≥ 4 coordinators to keys owned by the victim — need only
MODIFY on a blob table (`ModificationStatement.java:249`) and pass every default guardrail and size limit; the flush stalls
that let the queue fill are caused by the client's own ingest rate. The step no client can take is the budget: the OOM
needs `MaxDirectMemorySize` ≤ 640 MiB, which only an operator sets and which the shipped start-up script never resolves
for a 2 GiB-heap node. Hence `given <condition>`, rare. Condition H (hints off) adds an operator setting and gives the
same label.
